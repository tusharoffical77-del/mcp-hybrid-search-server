Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can expense business travel, client meals, and approved
software subscriptions. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 60 days of the expense
date through the finance portal. Manager approval is required for any
single expense exceeding $500. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at
client dinners, capped at $75 per person.
